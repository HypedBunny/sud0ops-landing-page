# Search engines, AI search and answer bots, and AI fetchers acting for a person may read every page
# except the contact API. AI training crawlers may not crawl at all.
#
# Content signals (Cloudflare's Content Signals Policy) say how the content may be used:
#   search   - building a search index and showing results
#   ai-input - feeding content to AI models to answer questions (retrieval, grounding)
#   ai-train - training or fine-tuning AI models
# A crawler follows only the group that names it, so each group carries the line.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/
